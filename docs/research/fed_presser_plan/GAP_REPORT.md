# Gap report: team_FINAL_PLAN.md vs our independent research (dated 2026-10-03)

Scope: every gap below was checked against `team_FINAL_PLAN.md` (the team's hardened plan, pre-registered family
called LOCKED), our `plan_v0.md`, our research notes and our three empirical studies. 59 candidate gaps came out of
four review lenses (data, models, statistics, execution) and one independent verification pass; one more was found
while merging (A-36). None was rejected outright: 37 held as stated, 22 were real but needed their amendment
corrected (verdict "partial"), and duplicates were merged. The table lists the 36 merged amendments that are
written into `merged_plan_v0.md`, section F.

Path shorthand used here and in the merged plan:

- `plan/` = `<solo-repo>/research/fed_presser_plan/`
- `hpg/` = `<solo-repo>/research/fed_presser_hpg/`
- `<scratch>/` = this research session's local scratchpad,
  `%LOCALAPPDATA%/Temp/<session-tool>/D--AUTOMATION/<session>/scratchpad/`
  (research notes `fedtalk/`, studies `intraday_study/`, `auction/`, `fedspeak/` replicate data only)

Rules applied: no locked hypothesis is changed silently. Each amendment is typed as a **correction** (a factual
error or a mis-implementation of the stated intent), an **implementation detail** (fixes a fork without changing the
hypothesis), a **clarifying amendment** (the locked text is ambiguous or self-contradictory, so the team must ratify
one reading, dated and blind), a **separately pre-registered extension** (own id, sample and decision rule, filed
before results; cannot replace or rescue a locked result), a **diagnostic** (reports only), or **new data / needs user
approval** (paid data or an action only the user may take).
